Questions

Frequently asked

Which section do I apply under?

It depends on why you want the firearm — self-defence, hunting, sport shooting or business use each fall under a different section of the Firearms Control Act. Use the finder above, or message us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the right one for you.

What documents do I need before I start?

Have your South African ID and any documents relevant to your licence type (such as competency certificates or club membership) on hand. The form you select will list exactly what's required for that category.

How long does the SAPS process take?

Processing time is set by SAPS and varies by province, station and licence type, so we can't promise a fixed date. Once your application is lodged, we follow up on your behalf and keep you posted on progress.

Can I apply if my existing licence has already expired?

Renewal applications are handled through the Firearm Renewal form. If your licence has already lapsed, message us on WhatsApp first — the process differs slightly depending on how long it's been expired.
